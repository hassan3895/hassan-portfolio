I'm Hassan, a motivated and detail-oriented frontend web developer based in Karachi, Pakistan. I enjoy transforming ideas into modern, functional websites with clean interfaces, responsive layouts and smooth user experiences.

Alongside web development, I have practical experience as an AI Virtual Assistant — managing Shopify stores, uploading and organizing products, and using ChatGPT and other AI tools to create SEO-friendly content for e-commerce brands.

I'm a fast learner who is genuinely passionate about technology, and I continuously sharpen my skills by building real-world projects and self-learning new tools.

Looking ahead, my goal is to master modern frontend frameworks, take on freelance projects with confidence, and become a well-rounded developer capable of delivering complete, polished digital solutions on every device.
